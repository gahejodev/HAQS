About this url slug generator

A URL slug is the readable part of a page address that identifies a page. Keep it concise and descriptive. Avoid changing established URLs without planning a redirect.

Frequently asked question

Should I include every word?

Usually keep it concise and descriptive. Include the main subject and remove unnecessary filler when that makes the URL clearer.

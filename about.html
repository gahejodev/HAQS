ABOUT

Useful tools without the clutter.

HAQS is a collection of practical browser-based utilities for writers, creators, students and website owners.

Our approach

We aim to make everyday tasks faster with clear interfaces and simple explanations. Current tools run locally in your browser and do not require an account. Generated suggestions are starting points, not professional guarantees.

Who makes HAQS?

HAQS is an independent project by Gahejo Dev. Visit the GitHub project to share feedback.

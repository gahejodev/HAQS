About this keyword density checker

This calculator counts exact consecutive phrase matches and calculates matches multiplied by phrase word count divided by total words. It is a descriptive metric, not a ranking target.

Frequently asked question

Does Google recommend a specific keyword density?

No universal keyword-density percentage guarantees rankings. Use this tool to inspect repetition, not chase a fixed number.

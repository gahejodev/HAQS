How to count words accurately in essays, articles and posts

A word count is a quick way to check whether a piece of writing fits an assignment, editorial brief or platform limit. The important part is using a consistent method.

What is included in a word count?

Most everyday word counters treat a word as a group of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. The exact result can vary between apps because hyphens, apostrophes, symbols, URLs and headings may be handled differently. If a school, publisher or client provides its own counting rules, follow those rules rather than assuming every counter will match.

How to check your text

  1. Copy the complete text you want to measure.
  2. Paste it into the free HAQS word counter.
  3. Review words, characters, sentences and paragraphs.
  4. Compare the result with the limit or brief you were given.
  5. If the count is close to a strict limit, check it in the destination platform too.

Word count vs. character count

Word count measures separated word units. Character count measures individual letters, numbers, punctuation marks and spaces. A platform that limits a caption or message by characters needs a character count, not a word count. The HAQS counter shows both total characters and characters without spaces so you can choose the relevant figure.

How can you shorten writing without losing meaning?

  • Remove repeated points and filler phrases.
  • Replace long expressions with clear, shorter wording.
  • Prefer specific verbs over vague phrases such as “make a decision”.
  • Keep examples that explain the point; remove examples that merely repeat it.
  • Read the revised text once for clarity, not just length.

Does a longer article rank better on Google?

There is no universal ideal word count that guarantees a search ranking. Write enough to satisfy the reader's actual question, and avoid adding paragraphs just to reach an arbitrary number. A concise answer can be more useful than a padded article.

Frequently asked questions

Do hyphenated words count as one word?

Counting rules differ. Some counters split hyphenated expressions while others treat them as one. Use the rules of the assignment or publishing platform when they are specified.

Can I count text on my phone?

Yes. HAQS runs in a modern mobile browser. Paste your text into the counter and the totals update as you type.

Is my text uploaded?

The HAQS counter performs its calculations in your browser. It does not need an account or a text-processing API.
